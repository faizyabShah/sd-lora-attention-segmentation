## 1. Contributions as worded in the draft (`sec/1_intro.tex:11–14`)

1. "A training-free pipeline that turns diffusion attention into class masks. It weights cross-attention layers by their agreement with self-attention, propagates the resulting seed from coarse to fine self-attention, and caps each finer level by the coarser one."
2. "Controlled ablations that isolate each choice: CA scales and layer weighting, coarse versus fine SA, the one-sided cap versus exact mass conservation and no constraint, and DenseCRF on each kind of map. Each is backed by quantitative and qualitative evidence."
3. "An oracle threshold analysis showing that the hierarchy improves calibration across images rather than ranking within an image, which explains its main failure case."
4. "A fold-safe selection protocol, with every choice made on VOC-Sim folds and the negative results it produced reported in full."

Numbering in the table below: main paper as printed (Sec. 5.3, Table 3, Fig. 4). Supplementary items carry an "S" prefix (Sec. S5, Tab. S7, Fig. S6) for the number printed in `supplementary.pdf`. The supplementary itself prints them without a prefix (see Entry 6, item 3.13).

### Notes on the table

- **Contribution 1's key number has two printed values.** 0.829/0.696 (abstract, intro, Tab. 1, Tab. 5) come from one run (v3) and 0.830/0.697 (Sec. 5.3, Tab. 3) from another (v2). Entry 6, item 1.1, recommends v3 as canonical.
- **"Out-of-fold" vs "forced out-of-fold".** The abstract and intro call 0.829 "out-of-fold". Protocol Section 5a.2 defines the reported OOF as that of the *selection procedure*, which kept fine-only (0.8230). The 0.829 is a forced OOF of an owner-adopted variant (Section 135). Tab. 5 and Tab. S10 label it correctly.
- **The "qualitative evidence" claim in contribution 2 holds unevenly.** Cap vs conservation (Fig. 4) and DenseCRF (Fig. 5) have main-paper visuals. CA scales and weighting have visuals only in the supp (Fig. S4). Coarse vs fine SA has no side-by-side visual of a fine-only map anywhere.
- **Contribution 3's "main failure case".** The abstract and Discussion describe it as "objects that fill the frame". Supp Sec. S5 and protocol Section 132 say frame-filling objects in general *gain* from the cap, and sheep is the exception (Entry 6, item 3.2).
- **Contribution 4's "every choice made on VOC-Sim folds".** This is true for where choices were evaluated. Two components were adopted by owner decision after the registered rule kept a simpler reference (Sec. 4 "Selection status"; Section 135). JC #1 asks for this to be handled carefully. The main paper has no table for contribution 4.
