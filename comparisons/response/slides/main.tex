\begin{frame}{Comment 1: the problem is that the override saw COCO-Cap}
\status{done}
\begin{itemize}
\item The decision to adopt the hierarchy was taken after COCO-Cap was scored, citing its +0.034 (protocol log, 31 Oct).
\item Yet the intro says ``Every choice is made on VOC-Sim folds, with COCO-Cap used only after freezing'' (p.~1).
\item Contribution 4 claims a protocol that ``made every choice''; the two novel choices were not made by it.
\item 0.829 is a forced out-of-fold score of the owners' variant; the procedure's own score is 0.823.
\item A reviewer can read this as choosing the method on the test set.
\end{itemize}
\src{protocol Section 135; paper abstract and Sec.~1 (p.~1)}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Comment 1: how we handle it}
\status{planned}
\begin{enumerate}
\item State what the rule chose and why, and print it next to BRACE on both benchmarks.
\item State that the override came after COCO-Cap; use COCO-Cap for the calibration analysis, not as held-out confirmation.
\item Label 0.829 ``forced out-of-fold'' everywhere.
\item Reword contribution 4: a protocol that records every decision, including the two overrides.
\item Cite a test scored after the decision was frozen: new backbones (comment 5), where the hierarchy holds on SD 2.1 and fails on SDXL.
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Comment 1: proposed contributions}
\status{planned}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A diffusion-only, training-free pipeline: SA-guided CA layer weighting, coarse-to-fine SA propagation, one-sided cap.
\item The scale structure matters in clutter, not on single objects: +0.006 per component on VOC-Sim, +0.034 and +0.018 on COCO-Cap.
\item An oracle threshold analysis: the gain is cross-image calibration, which also explains the main failure.
\item A protocol that records every decision, including the two overrides and their timing, plus paired reruns of competing methods.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}
\item To decide: is ``best diffusion-only method, CLIP methods in a separate block'' an acceptable headline?
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Comment 4: the optimized tokens never saw a test image}
\status{done}
\begin{itemize}
\item OVAM released 57 per-image tokens (2--3 per class), optimized on 63 separate generated training images.
\item No shared class and seed with any of the 500 VOC-Sim rows; no identical pixels.
\item No shared seed with COCO-Cap, whose prompts are captions, not ``a photograph of a [class]''.
\item So the block is few-shot (57 annotated images), not training-free, with no leakage.
\end{itemize}
\src{workplan Entry 5; \texttt{comparisons/notes/entry\_4\_token\_provenance.md}}
\end{frame}
