Class-macro mIoU. *VOC-sel.* = 5-fold OOF on VOC-Sim, full-VOC-Sim best threshold unchanged on COCO-Cap. Δ = row minus the row it builds on (column *vs*), paired image bootstrap 95% interval (2000). ## Notes (added after the report ran)

- **Capture.** 4 shards on renyi A100s (job 253917, 40 min), 2-image smoke first (253896, `_smoke`, kept).
  - The A3 seed equalled the frozen runtime's seed on every image; the job stops otherwise.
- **Scoring.**
  - VOC-Sim used `t1_ladder.score_one` on the full grid.
  - For COCO-Cap, DenseCRF counts were computed only at the thresholds the report reads: 0.45, 0.55 and each map's
    full-VOC-Sim best (0.425 for row 7, 0.45 for rows 5 and 6, 0.55 for row 8). Every other COCO-Cap DenseCRF
    threshold is stored as −1 and is never read (`../fast_score.py`).
  - No reported number depends on the skipped thresholds.
- **Reproduction of the paper's VOC-Sim screens at 0.45.**
  - Here: .667, .696, (.757), .790, .803, .816, .821.
  - Paper: .665, .691, (.756), .789, .800, .813, .817.
  - Rows 7 and 8 (VOC-sel.): .825 / .832 here vs the paper's .823 / .829.
- **Agreement with side F's runs/25** (a64aff6, V100), at 0.55:
  - row 7: .796 / .698 here vs F's 0.796 / 0.698;
  - row 8: .833 / .708 here vs F's 0.832 / 0.708.
  - These images were generated on A100s and are not bit-identical to the V100 captures.
- **The draft's +0.034, reproduced.** At VOC-sel., row 8 − row 7 on COCO-Cap is +0.036 [+0.024, +0.046]; the two rows
  use different thresholds (0.55 vs 0.425). At the shared 0.55 the same Δ is +0.010 [−0.005, +0.026] (workplan
  Entry 9).

  12.2 cm at `\footnotesize`.
