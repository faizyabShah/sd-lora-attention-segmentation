\paragraph{Modules, heads and operator.}
\owner{Claude (package B)}
Seeded by the frozen CA map, one propagation pass through all sixteen
self-attention modules raises in-sample mIoU from 0.696 (seed alone) to
0.755. Propagating through a single module,
\texttt{up\_blocks.3.attentions.2} (64$\times$64), is better still and is
chosen in all five folds (OOF 0.755 to 0.788); the exhaustive search over all
65{,}535 module subsets, which is not eligible for selection, picks the same
module. Restricting every module to heads $\{1, 4, 6\}$ is chosen in all
folds with a small gain (0.755 to 0.761, the conditional band). Propagating
with the transpose, $\sa^\top\ca$, gives 0.474, far below $\sa\ca$, which
confirms the row-stochastic orientation.
\source{protocol Section 30; evidence A4}

\paragraph{Combined confirmation and joint audit.}
\owner{Claude (SA-C text, protocol Section 29); Codex ran SA-C and the joint audit}
SA-C re-scores the four promoted SA changes (call 30 only, resolutions
8 and 64 only, module 15 only, heads $\{1, 4, 6\}$) in every unique
combination. Module 15 alone, with all calls and heads, is chosen in all five
folds: OOF mIoU rises from 0.756 to 0.789 (paired $\Delta$ $+0.042$,
$[+0.037, +0.047]$; 403 images better, 87 worse). It helps at all three
thresholds and raises AP from 0.954 to 0.956. None of the other restrictions
survives: adding the head subset to module 15 changes mIoU by $-0.002$, and
adding call 30 to that by $-0.005$. Eighteen of twenty classes improve;
\emph{person} falls by 0.055.

Because CA and SA were chosen in sequence, a further audit crosses all six
CA-C cells with all twelve SA-C cells and a no-SA control (78 configurations)
under the same rule. The sequential pair is kept in all five folds, and module
15 alone is the best SA partner for every CA seed. The closest alternative
(also removing CA head~1) is $+0.0017$ in-sample, with a paired interval that
includes zero.
\source{protocol Sections 33, 35, 36, 38; evidence A6}
