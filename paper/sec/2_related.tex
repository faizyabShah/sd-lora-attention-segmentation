\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Attention maps as segmentations.}
DAAM aggregates diffusion CA over layers and steps to attribute image regions to prompt words~\cite{tang2023daam}. OVAM adds a separate attribution prompt, so that any word can be queried, fuses CA with 64-pixel SA, refines with DenseCRF, and optionally optimizes tokens on annotated images~\cite{marcosmanchon2024ovam}. It also released VOC-Sim and COCO-Cap, the benchmarks we use. We keep its class-word setting and its DenseCRF, and change how CA is aggregated and how SA is applied across scales.

\paragraph{Selecting and weighting attention.}
GoCA weights CA layers by the agreement between their features and a self-attention structure~\cite{meng2026making}; our self-attention-guided layer weighting adapts this idea to every denoising call and a class-word query. NERVE weights heads by semantic output and propagates with a stopped random walk~\cite{mahatha2025nerve}. We tested adaptations of both in our setting (supplementary material): the layer weighting improved the final mask, while the head weighting and the random walk did not.

\paragraph{Propagation over self-attention graphs.}
SA is a row-stochastic affinity, so repeated application spreads a seed and eventually leaks across boundaries. SPARK sharpens the affinity by expansion, inflation and pruning~\cite{mahatha2026spark}. M2N2 treats SA as a Markov chain and uses arrival times~\cite{karmann2025m2n2}. PEARL studies attention geometry for training-free segmentation~\cite{pei2026pearl}. Multi-resolution use of SA appears in FA-Seg, which fuses refinements from several resolutions with fixed weights~\cite{che2025faseg}, and in SeeDiff, which re-extracts seeds between scales~\cite{park2025seediff}. Our hierarchy instead passes one map from coarse to fine. Its key ingredient, the one-sided cap, makes the coarse level an upper bound on what the finer level may claim. We compare it directly with exact (two-sided) mass conservation.

\paragraph{Boundary refinement.}
DenseCRF combines a unary prior with appearance-dependent pairwise potentials~\cite{krahenbuhl2011crf}. Attention pipelines apply it with fixed parameters~\cite{marcosmanchon2024ovam}; we do the same and analyze when it helps.
