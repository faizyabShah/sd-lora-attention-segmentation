\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Attention maps of diffusion models.}
Cross-attention maps of text-to-image diffusion models have been used to
explain and edit generated images \todo{[cite: Prompt-to-Prompt; DAAM ---
verify]}, and to segment the objects they depict without training
\todo{[cite: DAAM; DiffSegmenter; OVAM --- verify]}. OVAM generalizes the
attribution to arbitrary words through a separate attribution prompt and fuses
cross-attention with a self-attention map \todo{[cite: OVAM --- verify]}. We
score the released masks and heatmaps of OVAM, DAAM and Attn2Mask with the
procedures of OVAM's evaluation code, and use OVAM's separate attribution
prompt for COCO-Cap.

\paragraph{Self-attention as a grouping signal.}
Self-attention of diffusion models groups pixels into coherent regions. DiffSeg
merges attention maps by their similarity, and DiffCut partitions diffusion
features with a normalized cut \todo{[cite: DiffSeg; DiffCut --- verify]};
iterative self-attention refinement of a cross-attention seed has also been
proposed \todo{[cite: iSeg --- verify]}. Our propagation $\sa\ca$ is of this
kind, and is related to classical graph-based label propagation and random
walks \todo{[cite: Zhou et al.\ 2003; Grady 2006 --- verify]}. Rather than
proposing a new operator, we ask which propagation choices survive a
controlled selection, and why.

\paragraph{Generative data and grounding.}
DatasetDM, Grounded Diffusion and DiffuMask train additional modules on
synthetic or annotated data to produce masks from a generator
\todo{[cite: DatasetDM; Grounded Diffusion; DiffuMask --- verify]}. They are
not training-free; we report their released masks as reference points and
mark them as such.

\paragraph{Learned tokens.}
Textual inversion learns a word embedding for a concept from a few images
\todo{[cite: Textual Inversion --- verify]}. OVAM learns a class token in the
text-encoder output space against annotated masks \todo{[cite: OVAM ---
verify]}. We use OVAM's released tokens in a separately reported few-shot
track and show that each is an average of two or three per-image tokens.

\paragraph{Refinement and selection.}
DenseCRF is a standard post-processing step for coarse masks \todo{[cite:
Kr\"ahenb\"uhl and Koltun 2011 --- verify]} and is part of OVAM's pipeline. We
are not aware of a cross-validated, combination-confirmed selection of
diffusion-attention settings \todo{[verify by literature search before
claiming]}.
