\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
A diffusion model that draws ``a photograph of a sheep'' must decide, at every denoising step, which image positions belong to the word \emph{sheep} and which positions belong together. The first decision is visible in cross-attention (CA) between image queries and text keys; the second in self-attention (SA) between image positions. Reading both out yields a segmentation mask of the generated image without any training~\cite{tang2023daam,marcosmanchon2024ovam}, which makes generated images a source of free pixel labels.

Turning attention into masks is nevertheless a design problem: Stable Diffusion~1.5 exposes 31 denoising calls, four spatial scales, sixteen attention layers of each type and eight heads per layer. Existing pipelines average CA over modules, propagate it once with fine SA and refine the result~\cite{marcosmanchon2024ovam}. Recent work adds re-weighting, graph sharpening or random walks on top of that recipe~\cite{meng2026making,mahatha2025nerve,mahatha2026spark,park2025seediff}. We tested many such operators under a strict protocol, and most did not help. What did help was respecting the different roles that attention plays at different \emph{scales} (Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}). Coarse CA marks the object reliably and fine CA is noisy, so we weight CA layers by how well their spatial grouping agrees with self-attention. Coarse SA covers the whole object but blurs its boundary, and fine SA is precise but misses parts. We therefore propagate from coarse to fine and let each finer level redistribute evidence only \emph{within} the mass its parent assigns, never beyond it (a one-sided cap). DenseCRF then aligns the result with image edges.

The resulting pipeline uses no training, no optimized tokens and no tuned graph parameters. On VOC-Sim it reaches 0.829 out-of-fold mIoU, and on COCO-Cap it reaches 0.696 in frozen transfer, against 0.704 and 0.582 for OVAM with class words~\cite{marcosmanchon2024ovam}. Every choice is made on VOC-Sim folds, with COCO-Cap used only after freezing.

Our contributions are as follows.
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item A training-free pipeline that turns diffusion attention into class masks. It weights cross-attention layers by their agreement with self-attention, propagates the resulting seed from coarse to fine self-attention, and caps each finer level by the coarser one.
\item Controlled ablations that isolate each choice: CA scales and layer weighting, coarse versus fine SA, the one-sided cap versus exact mass conservation and no constraint, and DenseCRF on each kind of map. Each is backed by quantitative and qualitative evidence.
\item An oracle threshold analysis showing that the hierarchy improves calibration across images rather than ranking within an image, which explains its main failure case.
\item A fold-safe selection protocol, with every choice made on VOC-Sim folds and the negative results it produced reported in full.
\end{itemize}
