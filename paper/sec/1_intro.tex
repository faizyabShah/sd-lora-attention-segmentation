\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Text-to-image diffusion models localize the concepts they draw. The
cross-attention between image positions and prompt tokens marks where a word
is rendered, and the self-attention between image positions groups pixels that
belong together \todo{[cite: Prompt-to-Prompt; DAAM --- verify]}. Several
training-free segmenters read these maps out directly, and OVAM extends them
to words that are absent from the prompt and to tokens optimized on a few
annotated images \todo{[cite: OVAM; DiffSegmenter; DiffSeg --- verify]}.

Such methods expose many settings: which denoising calls, resolutions, layers
and heads to aggregate, how to combine cross- and self-attention, how to
normalize and threshold the map, and how to refine it. When these choices are
made on the images used for evaluation, the reported numbers are optimistic.
In our own exploratory phase, a pipeline tuned in this way reached 0.800 mIoU
on VOC-Sim, in-sample. Under held-out selection, several of its tuned choices
(fixed head subsets, a late window of denoising calls, a subset of
cross-attention layers) are not selected; the frozen pipeline is simpler and
reaches 0.813 out-of-fold.

We therefore study diffusion-attention segmentation under a controlled
protocol. Each setting is varied one axis at a time from an inclusive
reference, chosen on four of five class-stratified folds and scored on the
fifth, and frozen only after a combined confirmation in which separately
promoted changes are re-tested together. COCO-Cap is never used to choose
anything and serves only to measure transfer.

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A controlled selection protocol} for diffusion-attention
  segmentation, and the finding that most single-axis restrictions that pass
  their own screen, including head, call and resolution subsets, fail when
  combined (\cref{sec:selection,sec:ca,sec:sa}).
\item \textbf{A training-free pipeline}, frozen under that protocol: the
  cross-attention seed without the $32\times32$ modules, two passes through a
  single $64\times64$ self-attention module, and DenseCRF. It scores 0.813
  out-of-fold on VOC-Sim and 0.637 on COCO-Cap without any COCO-Cap tuning,
  above every released class-word baseline (\cref{sec:fusion,sec:transfer}).
\item \textbf{A few-shot variant} that replaces the class word by OVAM's
  optimized token: 0.864 on VOC-Sim and 0.685 on COCO-Cap. We also find that
  OVAM's released tokens average two or three per-image tokens, each learned
  from one annotated image (\cref{sec:fusion}).
\item \textbf{A mechanism analysis} of propagation: a second pass fills
  objects that the seed under-covers, a better seed makes it unnecessary, and
  in cluttered scenes its leakage outweighs the fill
  (\cref{sec:discussion}).
\end{itemize}
