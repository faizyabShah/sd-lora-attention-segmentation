\section{Reproducibility and audits}
\label{sec:supp_reproducibility}
\paragraph{Environments.}
Every shard records its commit and software environment. Packages ran in one of two conda environments, \texttt{gen} (PyTorch 2.4.1) and \texttt{trdp} (PyTorch 2.6 and NumPy 1.26 at the time of the runs), on V100 or A100 GPUs. Rule R7 requires one environment per package, and every comparison in the paper is within one package. Two exceptions are known: SG22--SG24 shards ran on a mix of V100 and A100 nodes, and the SG17, SG18 and SG21 shards do not record their environment. Regenerating images in another environment changes per-image counts slightly through floating-point differences in attention; the same two-pass-plus-DenseCRF pipeline, for example, scores 0.8132--0.8135 forced OOF across packages. We therefore never compare cells across packages.

\paragraph{Independent replication.}
Each package was reviewed by a second analyst, who re-derived its decisions from the per-image shards before the result entered the protocol; selected packages were also regenerated independently. The B2 token packages were independently regenerated (protocol Section 65), and the token optimizer was audited against OVAM's released code, with one recipe deviation recorded (Section 69). For SG17--SG27, every report was regenerated from the committed per-image shards: the regenerated tables match the committed ones byte for byte apart from line endings and last-digit floating-point differences, and every number quoted in the protocol matches. One dependence was found. SG23's raw promotion of MID-SG2 depends on the order in which tied candidates are visited, which the registration did not fix: the finalizer's order gives 5/5 fold votes, a neutral order 4/5, and the reverse order 3/5, in which case the reference is retained. SG23 is therefore marked as tie-order sensitive in Fig.~2 and Tab.~\ref{tab:supp_families}. The same check found no effect of tie order on SG22.

\paragraph{The A3 affinity orientation.}
SG24's A3 score compares each CA module's query--key affinity with a resized SA affinity. In the implementation (\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}segmentation/\allowbreak{}sg24\_\allowbreak{}ca.py}), the resized SA affinity is not transposed back, so the score is $\langle M^\top, P\rangle$ rather than GoCA's $\langle M, P\rangle$~\cite{meng2026making}. We measured the effect on four images: the per-call module weights change by 0.9--2.0\% (relative) and the call-averaged weights by at most 0.005. All A3 numbers in the paper are those of the implemented operator; a corrected replay of the A3 control is planned.
