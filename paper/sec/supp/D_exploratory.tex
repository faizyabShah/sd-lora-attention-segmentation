\section{Exploratory record}
\label{sec:supp_exploratory}
Before the fold protocol existed, seventeen exploratory experiments (B1--B17) chose settings on the same images they reported. They motivated the controlled screens but are never used as evidence in the paper: CA time windows and resolutions (B1), single and leave-out CA layers (B2), joint time and resolution (B3), the exhaustive 65{,}535 CA layer subsets (B4), threshold and normalization (B5), heads, time--layer, fusion and cleanup (B6), SA screens and progressive SA (B7--B10), component ablation and COCO-Cap transfer of an earlier final method (B11--B13), CA call windows (B14), CA-only screens (B15), OVAM's released masks (B16), and OVAM's optimized token with DenseCRF (B17). Their maxima are optimistic by construction. Their tables and figures are in Sec.~\ref{sec:supp_record_exploratory}, and Table~\ref{tab:record_ledger} lists each with the controlled screen that later tested the same idea.
