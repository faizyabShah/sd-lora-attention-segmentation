\section{Additional qualitative figures}
\label{sec:supp_gallery}
Figure~\ref{fig:supp_gallery} extends the failure columns of Fig.~1 by a fixed rule: the four classes with the lowest class IoU of the training-free pipeline on each dataset, each shown by its median-IoU image. No image was inspected before the choice. Two of the eight (VOC dining table and COCO person) also appear in Fig.~1. The COCO-Cap columns show three different failures. For the chair, propagation leaks into the wooden wall behind it. For the dining table, which fills the frame, the mask covers the plate and bread on it but not its surface. For the bottle, the mask covers both bottles, but only one is annotated, so part of the error is in the label rather than the mask.

\begin{figure*}[t]

\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/supp_gallery.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{The weakest classes, one median image each.} Rows as in Fig.~1: generated image; CA seed; two SA passes; final mask after DenseCRF at 0.45 (blue) with the ground-truth outline (yellow) and the image IoU. Columns: the four lowest class IoUs on VOC-Sim, then on COCO-Cap, from the lowest.}
\label{fig:supp_gallery}
\end{figure*}
