\section{Propagation, fusion and refinement}
\label{sec:fusion}

\paragraph{Propagation.}
\owner{Codex (registry Section 1.1)}
Applying each self-attention observation twice before averaging,
$\operatorname{mean}_i(\sa_i^2\ca)$, is selected in four of five folds; the
remaining fold selects three passes.  The registered selection procedure
improves OOF mIoU from 0.788 to 0.799.  Evaluating the frozen two-pass choice
in every held-out fold gives 0.800 mIoU ($+0.012$), with paired mean image-IoU
$+0.014$ and 95\% interval $[+0.011,+0.018]$ (365 images better, 125 worse).
It particularly improves \emph{chair} ($+0.078$) and \emph{person}
($+0.030$), the latter having lost 0.055 when one-pass SA was introduced.

Averaging affinities before taking powers is not selected, showing that
propagating separately through each call--head observation preserves useful
structure.  Restart propagation is also rejected under both aggregation
orders.  Row-wise top-$k$ sparsification degrades monotonically as fewer edges
are retained and remains below the dense reference at $k=1024$.  The two-pass
operator advances to combined confirmation.
\source{protocol Sections 37 and 43; registry P1, W3, P2, P6}

\paragraph{Fusion and calibration.}
\owner{Claude (registry Section 1.2)}
On the frozen pair, we test mixing the propagated map with the seed
($\lambda\,\sa\ca + (1-\lambda)\,\ca$), gating ($\ca \odot \sa\ca$) and its
geometric mean, three normalizations (percentile, maximum, per-image Otsu
threshold), and OVAM's DenseCRF on the thresholded map. Each method chooses its
parameter and mask threshold on the training folds, as does the reference.
None of the mixing, gating or normalization variants is chosen in any fold:
per-image Otsu and maximum normalization raise mIoU slightly, but in every
fold they either lose more than 0.05 IoU on a class (\eg \emph{sheep}
$-0.07$) or improve fewer than 12 classes, which the promotion rule forbids. DenseCRF is chosen in all five folds and raises OOF mIoU from
0.788 to 0.805 (paired $\Delta$ $+0.022$, $[+0.019, +0.026]$; 361 images
better, 129 worse). It helps at every threshold and improves all twenty
classes.
\source{protocol Section 44; registry R2}
