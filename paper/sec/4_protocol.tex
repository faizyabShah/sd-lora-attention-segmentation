\subsection{Cross-validated selection}
\label{sec:selection}
\owner{Claude (protocol Section 29: statistical-selection wording)}

Every attention setting is chosen by the same cross-validated rule, so that
no reported gain comes from tuning on the images it is reported on.
\source{protocol Sections 5, 5a; registry R3--R5}

\paragraph{One axis at a time.}
Each screen varies one axis (calls, resolutions, modules, heads, or the
attribution convention) away from an \emph{inclusive reference} that keeps
every option of that axis, with all other settings held at their reference
values.

\paragraph{Folds.}
The 490 VOC-Sim images are split once into five folds of 98 images,
stratified by class (four or five images per class per fold) with a fixed
seed. For each held-out fold, a candidate is chosen using only the other four
folds and then scored on the held-out fold. Pooling the five held-out folds
gives an out-of-fold (OOF) mIoU for the selection procedure and for the
reference.

\paragraph{Promotion rule.}
On the training folds, a candidate replaces the reference if it improves
mIoU at the 0.45 threshold by at least $0.010$, or by $0.005$--$0.0099$ when at
least 12 of the 20 classes improve and no class loses more than $0.05$.
Candidates within $0.002$ of each other are resolved towards the more
inclusive setting. A setting is promoted only if at least four of the five
folds choose it \emph{and} its pooled OOF mIoU exceeds the reference's.

\paragraph{Combined confirmation.}
Settings promoted by separate one-axis screens are not assumed to combine.
They are re-scored together in a single run, alone and in every combination,
and the same rule chooses among them. Only this confirmation freezes a stage.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and calibration.}
We report a paired bootstrap (20{,}000 resamples) 95\% interval of the mean
per-image IoU change, the number of images that improve and worsen, the
change in mIoU at thresholds 0.35 and 0.55, and the mean average precision
(AP) of the continuous map. A change is \emph{calibration-dependent} if its
sign differs across the three thresholds.
