\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:exp}
\paragraph{Benchmarks and metric.}
We use the two benchmarks released with OVAM~\cite{marcosmanchon2024ovam}: VOC-Sim, with 490 annotated images of the 20 VOC classes from prompts ``a photograph of a [class]'', and COCO-Cap, with 707 annotated images generated from COCO captions, often cluttered and sometimes without the class name in the caption. We regenerate each image with Stable Diffusion~1.5 from its released prompt and seed (30 steps, guidance 7.5) to record attention. The metric is the mean over 20 classes of the class IoU, pooled over all images of the class.

\paragraph{Fold-safe selection.}
VOC-Sim is the only selection set. Its images are split once into five class-stratified folds. A design choice and its threshold are chosen on four folds and scored on the fifth. Pooling the five held-out folds gives the out-of-fold (OOF) estimate, and \emph{forced OOF} scores a named variant in every fold. A change was promoted when it gained at least 0.010 mIoU on the training folds (or at least 0.005 with 12 of 20 classes improved and no class losing more than 0.05), won at least four of five folds, and improved the pooled OOF score. COCO-Cap is scored once, with the method and threshold frozen on VOC-Sim. Every comparison in Secs.~\ref{sec:seed}--\ref{sec:calibration} is between cells of one run on the same generated images. Paired intervals are 95\% bootstrap intervals over images (20{,}000 resamples).

\paragraph{Selection status.}
The CA scales, the SA module, the two-pass baseline, DenseCRF and the SA call window were promoted by this rule. Two components were adopted after the rule had retained a simpler reference. The A3 weights won 4/5 folds and transferred, but the multi-candidate screen kept uniform weights. The capped hierarchy won 5/5 folds against both references, but one class (sheep, Sec.~\ref{sec:calibration}) lost more than the 0.05 guard. We label both as post-registration decisions and report the registered outcomes in full in the supplementary material, together with 45 registered families of alternatives, most of which failed.
