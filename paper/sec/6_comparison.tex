\section{Comparison with published methods}
\label{sec:comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:published} places the pipeline beside the class-word results reported by OVAM on the same benchmarks. With class words only and no training, it exceeds OVAM by 0.125 on VOC-Sim and 0.114 on COCO-Cap. It also exceeds DatasetDM, which trains an extra decoder, on both benchmarks. Most of the COCO-Cap margin over our fine-only pipeline comes from the capped hierarchy: cluttered scenes benefit most from a coarse envelope. Released baseline masks scored under our evaluation are in the supplementary material.
