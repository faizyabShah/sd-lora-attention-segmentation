\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
\paragraph{A coherent mechanism, not a monotone list of tricks.}
The CA resolution choice improves the operating-point semantic seed. A single 64-pixel SA module then spreads that seed more usefully than averaging every module. A second pass repairs under-covered objects, and image-aware refinement removes boundary errors. Crucially, these effects interact: head and call subsets that pass independent screens disappear after the relevant combination is tested. The later middle-call improvement appears only after R2. A3's improvement is likewise measured only for its implemented weighted seed and final pipeline, not as a generic CA-only gain; its CA-only mIoU actually falls in the SG24 screen.

\paragraph{What the figures do and do not show.}
The airplane CA panels in Fig.~\ref{fig:ca_example} illustrate the spatial signals entering the tested operator. The quantitative figure and Tab.~\ref{tab:stage_decisions} state the corresponding cohort-level evidence. The gain and loss in Fig.~\ref{fig:temporal_tradeoff} illustrate why a temporal restriction needs a cohort-level test. The second-pass diagnostic explains why deeper diffusion eventually leaks: among newly accepted pixels, true-positive rates decline sharply with arrival depth, particularly on COCO-Cap. These observations suggest targeted propagation as future work, but they do not prove a universal per-pixel confidence rule.

\paragraph{Selection and implementation boundaries.}
The main all-call pipeline and the later middle-call refinement were selected by their registered VOC rules. A3 received a favorable forced pairwise comparison after the multi-candidate selector kept A0; consequently it is exploratory and its full-VOC score is in-sample. An independent code audit found that its resized SA affinity was transposed relative to the GoCA-inspired equation. Until a corrected replay is evaluated, the A3 number belongs only to the implemented operator. SG25 and SG27 further reuse that seed and inherit this limitation.

The benchmarks contain images generated from prompts and seeds, not natural-image segmentation benchmarks. Our regenerated images may differ from the renders annotated by OVAM, and some published baselines used their own generation settings. For that reason, the final literature table is contextual, not a claim of a paired statistical victory. All choices are made on VOC-Sim and one diffusion backbone; the 20-class macro mean also hides class and image-level variation. A separate optimized-token track uses annotations and is not part of the training-free claim.

\section{Conclusion}
The most reliable training-free recipe in this study is a selective CA seed, observation-wise propagation through one fine SA module, two passes for object completion, and fixed image-aware refinement. Controlled VOC folds and frozen COCO transfer reveal both its gains and its failure mode: later propagation or additional scale can help object extent while leaking into unrelated regions. Reporting the registered selection path beside exploratory extensions gives a clearer account of what is established and what remains to be tested.
