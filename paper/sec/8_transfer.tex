\section{Transfer and published comparison}
\label{sec:transfer}
\emph{Question: does the VOC-selected system transfer, and does anything beyond it?} We now freeze every choice on VOC-Sim and score COCO-Cap. The confirmed all-call training-free pipeline gives 0.637 mIoU; the later joint temporal selection gives 0.644. COCO-Cap includes captions in which the queried class name is absent, so separate class-word attribution is important. Neither the temporal window nor the threshold is chosen on COCO-Cap.

\paragraph{Class-level transfer is weak.}
Aggregate gains transfer more reliably than their class pattern. Across fourteen within-run comparisons, the correlation between a change's per-class effect on VOC-Sim and on COCO-Cap ranges from $-0.01$ to $+0.46$ (supplementary material). The middle-call window, for instance, improves 16 VOC and 17 COCO classes, but VOC person loses (supplementary material). A class that a change helps on one benchmark is therefore weak evidence that it helps on the other.

\paragraph{Beyond the validated system.}
After SG22 the open question moved back to the seed and to SA scale; each study keeps the validated SA and DenseCRF. A CA module weighting (SG24-A) scores the agreement of each CA module's query--key affinity with a paired SA affinity. Its registered multi-candidate selector kept the unweighted reference; the A3 variant nonetheless scores 0.823 forced OOF versus 0.817 for the same-run reference, and 0.662 versus 0.644 on COCO-Cap, and was retained as the working default by owner decision. Its full-VOC threshold gives 0.824, which is in-sample and is not used as the main VOC estimate. Restoring the five 32-pixel CA modules under A3 (SG24-B) loses 0.8 forced-OOF VOC points and wins no fold, while gaining 0.8 on COCO-Cap.

The studies that follow, all on the A3 seed, share one pattern: coarser SA helps transfer and hurts selection. Propagating through a 16-pixel SA module before the 64-pixel one (SG25) raises frozen COCO-Cap from 0.662 to 0.698 and improves 19 of 20 COCO classes, yet scores 0.818 VOC OOF against 0.823 and improves only 9 VOC classes. Coarse-guided SA (SG26) repeats this: after DenseCRF, each new cell loses 0.4--2.5 forced-OOF VOC points and gains 1.3--2.0 COCO-Cap points. A 46-formula late-fusion screen (SG27) retains the fine-scale reference in all five folds; its best COCO cell, 0.705, loses VOC and sits at the edge of the threshold grid. A PEARL-inspired study (SG28, \cite{pei2026pearl}) found one boundary-aware Laplacian that gains 0.4 raw VOC points, but only $+0.0001$ after DenseCRF, so again the gain does not survive refinement. A NERVE-inspired study (SG29, \cite{mahatha2025nerve}) is running. These results suggest that COCO-Cap's cluttered scenes benefit from coarser context that the simpler VOC-Sim scenes do not reward. Under the registered rule, they do not change the selected system.
