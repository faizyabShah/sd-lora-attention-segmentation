\paragraph{Layers, heads and attribution.}
\owner{Claude (package B)}
Removing the two 32$\times$32 decoder modules
(\texttt{up\_blocks.2.attentions.0} and \texttt{.1}) is chosen in all five
folds and raises OOF mIoU from 0.666 to 0.692. Removing head~1 in every module
is chosen in four folds (0.666 to 0.674). Both changes are
calibration-dependent: they help at 0.45 and 0.55 but not at 0.35, and both
slightly lower AP, so they make the map more selective rather than rank
pixels better. Head~1 alone scores 0.098 mIoU, against 0.26--0.43 for every
other head. For the attribution convention, the reference (conditional query,
softmax over all 77 padded positions, mean over all class-word pieces) is kept
in all folds; OVAM's convention (unconditional query, unpadded softmax, first
piece) is 0.016 lower.
\source{protocol Section 21; evidence A1}

\paragraph{Combined confirmation.}
\owner{Claude (CA-C)}
Re-scoring the three promoted changes together (\cref{tab:controlled_path})
freezes the resolution change alone: without the five 32$\times$32 modules,
OOF mIoU rises from 0.665 to 0.691 (four of five folds; paired
$\Delta$ image IoU $+0.022$, 95\% interval $[+0.014, +0.029]$). It is not
calibration-dependent ($+0.001$, $+0.030$ and $+0.045$ at 0.35, 0.45 and
0.55), although mean AP falls from 0.922 to 0.913. The layer change is
contained in it and adds nothing. Removing head~1 on top of it changes mIoU by
only $+0.0004$, inside the tie band, so all eight heads are kept: a head
restriction that passes its own screen does not survive the combination.
\source{protocol Section 27; evidence A3}
