# Evidence: every experiment and how each setting was reached

Generated by `scripts/narmyn/attention/build_evidence.py` from the committed result files; nothing is re-run. mIoU is the 20-class macro mean of pooled foreground IoU unless stated.

**How to read it.** Experiments are in two tiers.

- **Tier B (B1–B17), exploratory.** Settings were chosen on the same images they are reported on (the 99 dev images are part of the 490). These numbers show how the method was found, not how well it generalizes.
- **Tier A (A1–A6), controlled.** Protocol v1.2: one axis at a time from a fixed reference, settings chosen on four folds and scored on the fifth, frozen only with 4 of 5 fold votes and an out-of-fold gain. Both CA packages are complete (A2 by Codex: calls and resolutions; A1 by Claude: layers, heads, attribution), and the combined confirmation (A3, CA-C) has frozen the CA configuration: calls 0–30, resolutions 8, 16 and 64 (the 32×32 modules removed), all 8 heads, conditional query, padded softmax, all class-word pieces. The SA screens (A4 by Claude, A5 by Codex) and the combined SA confirmation (A6, run by Codex) have frozen SA: one y = A x pass through SA module 15 (U3.2, 64×64) only, all calls 0–30, all 8 heads.

Full data: `all_configurations.csv.gz` (one row per configuration, all experiments), `controlled_ca_package_a.csv`, `controlled_ca_package_b.csv`, `controlled_ca_confirmation.csv`, `controlled_sa_package_a.csv`, `controlled_sa_package_b.csv` and `controlled_sa_confirmation.csv` (the 1,279 + 426 + 6 + 1,279 + 416 + 12 controlled configurations with per-fold held-out mIoU, thresholds 0.35/0.45/0.55 and AP), `ledger.csv`, `tables/*.md|tex`, `figures/*.png|pdf`.

## 8. Limits of this evidence

- Tier B settings were chosen on the reported images; only COCO-Cap is untouched by the earlier final method, and the revised one-pass system was informed by COCO-Cap.
- Tier A is complete for CA and SA (screens and both combined confirmations). The deferred phase (Section 8: repeated passes, fusion, calibration, cleanup, DenseCRF, tokens) and the COCO-Cap transfer of the frozen method are still to run.
- The frozen SA (module 15 only) lowers `person` IoU by 0.055; SA-T and SA-R were calibration-dependent and do not survive SA-C.
- The frozen CA change (CA-R) helps mainly at the 0.45 selection threshold and above and slightly lowers AP; CA-L and CA-H change sign at 0.35 (Fig. 3).
- CA-C ran on V100 GPUs, packages A and B on an A100; the identical reference differs by 0.0007 mIoU across them, so CA-C rows are compared only with each other.
- Released OVAM masks were made on the authors' renders; all other rows use regenerated images scored on the same annotations.
