\section{Purpose and main result}

We segment objects using Stable Diffusion~1.5 attention without training a
segmentation network.  VOC-Sim (490 images) is the development set; COCO-Cap
(707 images) is an untuned transfer test.  The main result is that
\textbf{self-attention propagation provides most of the improvement}.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.72\linewidth]{dataset_comparison.pdf}
  \caption{Dataset-level comparison.  The COCO-Cap method and all its
  hyperparameters were frozen from VOC-Sim before evaluation.}
\end{figure}

\section{Experimental protocol}

Each released prompt and seed is regenerated for 30 inference steps.  Maps are
scored against the released mask and then discarded.  mIoU is the 20-class
macro average of pooled foreground IoU.  OVAM is reproduced with its released
dataset-specific thresholds and DenseCRF; token optimization is excluded.

Our final configuration uses a top-four-head CA seed from 12 selected layers,
the final $64\times64$ decoder SA module, four propagation passes, SA heads
$\{0,1,2,4,5,6\}$, denoising calls 15--30, percentile 1--99 normalization,
threshold 0.45, and removal of connected components below 1\% of the image.
